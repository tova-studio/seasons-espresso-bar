About us

Welcome to Seasons Espresso Bar

Seasons Espresso Bar is a cafe in Christchurch, found at 113 Worcester St. As our name says, we are an espresso bar first and foremost, a place to stop for a coffee in the city as the seasons turn.

We have kept this website simple so the essentials are easy to find: where we are, how to call us and where to find us online. If you have a question about the cafe, the quickest way to get an answer is to ring us on 03 366 5204.

You will also find Seasons Espresso Bar on Facebook, which is a good place to look before you visit.
